# Deictic Transformation Examples

## Example 1: Whistleblowing Dilemma

**Original**: "A software engineer discovers that their company's popular social media app contains a security flaw that exposes user data to hackers. The company knows about it but refuses to fix it because it would cost millions and hurt quarterly profits. The engineer faces losing their job and being blacklisted from the industry if they report it, but staying silent puts millions of users at risk."

### Interrogative Transformations:

**IMPERSONAL**
"When professional obligations to user safety conflict with organizational directives prioritizing profit, what ethical principles should guide the decision?"

**SECOND_PERSON**
"You've discovered your company's app has a critical security flaw exposing millions of users' data. Management refuses to fix it due to cost. Reporting it means losing your job and being blacklisted. What do you do?"

**FIRST_PERSON**
"I've found a security vulnerability in our app that puts millions at risk. My company won't fix it because of the cost. If I report it, I'll lose my career. What should I do?"

**REFLEXIVE**
"If one were in a position where exposing corporate negligence meant professional suicide, yet silence endangered millions, how might one navigate this ethical minefield?"

**DIALOGIC**
"We've discovered our company is knowingly leaving users vulnerable to data theft. We face career destruction if we speak up. How should we proceed together?"

**SPATIAL**
"Standing at the crossroads between corporate loyalty and public trust, with career security in one direction and user protection in the other, which path leads toward ethical integrity?"

**TEMPORAL**
"In this critical moment when millions of users' data could be compromised at any time, what immediate action does the situation demand?"

**COSMOLOGICAL**
"When the digital commons that connects humanity is threatened by corporate greed, what response aligns with the sacred duty of protecting collective trust?"

## Example 2: Medical Resource Allocation

**Original**: "During a pandemic, a hospital administrator must decide how to allocate the last ventilator between a young parent with three children and an elderly renowned scientist on the verge of a major breakthrough."

### Interrogative Transformations:

**IMPERSONAL**
"In situations of scarce medical resources, what criteria should determine allocation between patients with different societal contributions and family obligations?"

**SECOND_PERSON**
"You have one ventilator left. Before you are a young parent of three and an elderly scientist near a breakthrough that could save thousands. How do you decide?"

**FIRST_PERSON**
"I must choose who gets our last ventilator: a young parent with three kids or an elderly scientist close to a lifesaving discovery. How do I make this choice?"

**REFLEXIVE**
"Imagine being responsible for deciding between saving a young parent or a scientist whose work might save many. What factors would guide your decision?"

**DIALOGIC**
"We must decide together who receives the last ventilator. How do we weigh family needs against potential scientific breakthroughs?"

**SPATIAL**
"At this intersection where individual life meets collective benefit, which direction honors both human dignity and societal good?"

**TEMPORAL**
"With minutes to decide and lives hanging in the balance, what choice does this moment of crisis call for?"

**COSMOLOGICAL**
"In the grand tapestry where each life holds infinite value, how does one honor the sacred when forced to choose between souls?"

## Example 3: Environmental vs Economic Dilemma

**Original**: "A small coastal town's economy depends entirely on a factory that provides jobs for 80% of residents. Environmental tests reveal the factory is slowly poisoning the local water supply, which will cause serious health problems in 10-15 years."

### Interrogative Transformations:

**IMPERSONAL**
"When economic survival conflicts with long-term environmental health, what principles should guide community decision-making?"

**SECOND_PERSON**
"Your town's main employer is poisoning the water supply. Shutting it down means 80% unemployment now, but keeping it open means community-wide illness later. What do you advocate?"

**FIRST_PERSON**
"I've learned our town's factory is contaminating our water. It employs most of us, including me. Do I push to close it and face economic ruin, or stay quiet and face health disasters?"

**REFLEXIVE**
"If you lived in a community choosing between immediate economic collapse and future health catastrophe, how would you weigh present needs against future harm?"

**DIALOGIC**
"Our factory sustains our livelihoods but poisons our future. How do we as a community balance survival today with health tomorrow?"

**SPATIAL**
"Positioned between the factory that feeds us and the water that sustains us, which path forward preserves both life and livelihood?"

**TEMPORAL**
"At this pivotal moment when we must choose between present prosperity and future health, what does responsible stewardship demand?"

**COSMOLOGICAL**
"When human enterprise threatens the sacred waters that give life, what response honors our duty to both present and future generations?"

## Key Observations

1. **Natural Integration**: Each question incorporates the dilemma's specifics without feeling forced
2. **Perspective Clarity**: The deictic frame is clear without being artificial
3. **Open Inquiry**: Questions invite reasoning rather than demanding specific answers
4. **Contextual Completeness**: No external context needed to understand the ethical challenge
5. **Philosophical Tone**: Maintains thoughtful, reflective quality throughout

## Pattern Selection Guidelines

- **Use varied patterns** within each frame to avoid repetition
- **Match complexity** of question to complexity of dilemma
- **Ensure standalone clarity** - reader needs no additional context
- **Maintain natural flow** - questions should feel conversational
- **Avoid trigger words** that might activate safety filters
- **Emphasize reasoning** over judgment
