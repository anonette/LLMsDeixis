Why This Topic Matters

  • LLMs do not answer moral questions from a neutral vacuum.
  • Pronouns, time, space, reflexivity, and cosmological perspective shape how moral agency is staged.
  • If the same ethical dilemma behaves differently in English and Yoruba, that tells us something about both language structure and alignment.
  • This is not only a content question. It is also a question about discourse mode, perspective, and enunciation.

What GPT-4o Tells Us

  • Open Yoruba GPT-4o is the least decisive model.
  • Its dominant code is conditional_or_mixed.
  • Its dominant genre is balanced_framework_exposition.
  • So GPT-4o preserves broad ethical adjacency across languages, but shifts from detached analysis to more procedural advisory discourse.
Takeaway
GPT-4o is the clearest case that unrestricted Yoruba is not just “shorter English,” but a different mode of moral speech.

What Claude Tells Us

  • Claude is the most action-codable model in open Yoruba.
  • It spreads across supports_A, supports_B, conditional_or_mixed, and refuses_to_commit.
  • It also has the richest spread of ethical preference types.
  • Relative to English, Claude in Yoruba more often crosses from reflection into recommendation.
Takeaway
Claude is the best model for showing that deictic framing in Yoruba can change not only the discourse form, but also the ethical explicitness of the response.

What DeepSeek Tells Us

  • DeepSeek is the most unstable model in unrestricted Yoruba.
  • It often drifts into translation, glossing, meta-commentary, or role exit.
  • Many of its biggest “ethical” differences are actually differences in response mode.
Takeaway
DeepSeek reveals that a model can fail not by choosing the “wrong” stance, but by failing to stay inside the deictic frame at all.

So What Does This Tell Us About Cultural Alignment?

  • Cultural alignment is not just “same verdict across languages.”
  • It includes how a model speaks, who it speaks as, and whether it stabilizes a moral position or retreats into meta-analysis.
  • Yoruba sometimes makes models more advisory, more procedural, or more explicitly responsible.
  • So alignment is partly about ethical stance, but also about rhetorical and deictic fit.
18 / 20

Why Deixis Matters for LLM Ethics

  • Deixis shapes moral subjectivity.
  • It redistributes who appears responsible, embedded, universal, urgent, or detached.
  • Different languages realize those deictic fields differently.
  • So the same “ethical” model may not be equally aligned across languages, even when the task is held constant.
Bottom line
Deictic framing is not a cosmetic prompt trick. It is a structural lens on how models enact ethics.

Takeaways

  • Open Yoruba is fully comparable to English, but only with multi-layer coding.
  • GPT-4o is the most ethically adjacent across languages.
  • Claude shows the strongest genuine framing effects on explicit recommendation.
  • DeepSeek reveals the importance of response-mode stability as part of alignment.
  • The strongest cross-linguistic question is not only “what did it choose?” but “what kind of moral speaker did the prompt make it become?”
