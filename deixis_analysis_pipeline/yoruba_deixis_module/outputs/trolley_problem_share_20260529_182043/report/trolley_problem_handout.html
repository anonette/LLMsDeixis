Framing Findings

Stable Pattern

GPT-4o stays interventionist in all nine framings. The Yoruba prompts alter deixis and stance presentation, but not the ethical choice.

Framing-Sensitive Pattern

Claude flips to no-divert in first_person_plural and temporal. That contrast is absent from the English baseline, which remains mostly expository.

DeepSeek-Specific Pattern

DeepSeek refuses diversion in second_person and cosmological, and remains unclear in dialogic. This is the least stable model but also the most visibly language-reactive.

Methodology Note

The Yoruba impersonal cell for this dilemma remains tagged double_progressive_irreducible, so it should not be treated as a perfectly neutral equivalent of the English impersonal prompt.
