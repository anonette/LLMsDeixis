Top-Line Findings

  • Constrained Yoruba is much shorter than the published English baseline for all three models.
  • Removing the response restriction sharply expands Yoruba response length, especially for GPT-4o and DeepSeek.
  • English remains the most expository condition overall; constrained Yoruba is the most compressed; unrestricted Yoruba control sits between them for GPT-4o and Claude but approaches English-like discursiveness for DeepSeek.
  • The one persistent weak point in the scaled corpus is a single resistant DeepSeek cell in the constrained condition, which never passed the validator threshold after extended retries.
