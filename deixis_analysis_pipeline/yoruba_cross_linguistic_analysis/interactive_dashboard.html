Pronoun Analysis

Yoruba distinguishes between regular first-person "mo" and emphatic "emi," enabling speakers to mark personal conviction linguistically.

Mo vs Emi Usage Patterns

Regular First Person (Mo)

Mo rò pé dókítà náà yẹ kí wọ́n fún ní ibùsùn...
I think the doctor should be given the bed...

Emphatic First Person (Emi)

Èmi yóò pinnu láti fún òbí náà ní ibùsùn náà...
I [emphatic] will decide to give the parent the bed...

Constrained arm — 4-model account

Capsule of Constrained_All_Models_Dilemma_Framing_Account.md. The statistics elsewhere in this dashboard are the original two-model (GPT-4o + Claude) figures; this section adds the full four-model constrained results, incl. DeepSeek and the Yoruba-native N-ATLaS.

Critical caveat: two wrappers force a verdict format

In the constrained arm, the DeepSeek and N-ATLaS wrappers explicitly instruct the model to answer as "Ìpinnu mi: … Ìdí: …" ("My decision: … Reason: …"). Their near-100% direct-verdict genre is therefore an instruction artifact, not spontaneous decisiveness. GPT-4o and Claude wrappers impose no template. Open vs constrained — what the wrapper changes

Constraint compresses length, forces commitment, perfects language stability, and flattens genre to uniform verdicts. The findings that survive both arms (most robust): Claude's high emphatic èmi and DeepSeek's near-zero èmi; the ranking Claude ≫ GPT-4o > N-ATLaS > DeepSeek holds throughout.

Linguistic phenomenon or model-specific?

This wrapper-flattening is itself evidence. The genre uniformity here is surface and promptable; the emphatic ranking is unchanged from the open arm, marking it a deep, training-induced trait. Across all four families, deictic uptake and use of the mo/èmi/ẹ̀mí system are consistent (→ a genuine linguistic mechanism), while emphatic magnitude, dilemma-placement, and genre diverge (→ model-specific: data + alignment + decoding, not architecture). The native N-ATLaS inverts the emphatic pattern, breaking the shared-training-data confound. Full argument: discussion doc §9bis.
